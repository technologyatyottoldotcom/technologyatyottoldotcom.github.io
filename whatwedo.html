What we do

We buy good businesses, and we keep them.

Two concentrated, long-only equity portfolios, one in India and one global. Held for years, not quarters, and run under a single code of conduct that puts your capital first.

How we invest

Three ideas, both strategies

1

Businesses, not tickers

We look at a company the way a private owner would: cash flows, market position, management, and whether it makes people's lives better.

2

Buy, then hold

We think in five-year horizons and longer. We don't trade, time the market or rotate between asset classes. A position changes only when the business does.

3

Fewer, better

Around a dozen companies per portfolio. Long only, no leverage, no shorting, and no sin stocks.

Stewardship

Nine promises we keep

Stewardship means looking after something on someone else's behalf, with their interests at heart. These are the terms on which we accept capital.

  1. We are partners. Most of our own wealth is invested alongside yours.
  2. Survive first. We aim for decent returns, not a roulette game.
  3. We buy to own. Not to flip. We grow with good companies, long term.
  4. We fix mistakes fast. When we are wrong, we act immediately.
  5. Full transparency. Every investor, large or small, gets the same information.
  6. Bad news first. Good news can always wait.
  7. No conflicted deals. We never take on incentives that cut against yours.
  8. Dignity in every dealing. Respect and care, for investors and team alike.
  9. No side deals. No confidential agreements and no special treatment for anyone.
