Objective

Long-term growth of your investment.

The investment team believes that high quality companies with dominant market positions, underpinned by powerful intangible assets, can generate attractive returns over the long term.

Approach

We invest in businesses, not a ticker. The largest potential value sits in cash-flow-generating, innovation-driven businesses making the earth a more sustainable place for future generations.

We also believe in responsible capitalism: capitalism in which stakeholder interests are largely aligned with shareholder interests, and profit maximisation is directly proportional to human value maximisation.

We broadly follow a natural ESG framework and avoid sin stocks.

Investment approach

Investment philosophy

Global opportunities focus

We invest with a global remit in companies which have cash flow generating innovative businesses and a significant moat in what they do.

Companies in our portfolio are typically market leaders in their respective niches with a long runway left in their innovation cycle.

Dominant market positions underpinned by powerful intangible assets, whether brands, networks or intellectual property, can compound value over decades.

Strategy synthesis

GreatBear is a long-only investor. Market irrationality about the price of a business may have no upper bound, and we do not like that exposure.

We look at a business with a private equity approach. Buying stock is an opportunity to grow with the fortunes of a company. Our time frame for analysis is never less than five years.

Effort to be broadly correct rather than precisely wrong.

Capital allocation is our main job.
